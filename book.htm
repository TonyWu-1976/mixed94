记得那是在 1996 年的春天 ...

   May   23rd


       The general blueprint came into being. Each fancy idea that popped up before us was at first sight dumbfounding but soon enough welcomed with applause. We thought of collecting every sparkling bit in the whole process and putting it down for commemoration. We even planned to find a safe place for it on the Laohe Hill and then draw a guidance map to be published together with the book. You might think it was mere joking, but we really regard it invaluable and even sacred. Who knows we won't do what we've said.

   June   4th


       The most serious problem we are facing is money. But what more depressing are those disapprovals we learnt from the opinion poll. We are confused.
       Looking forward: we can conceive the ultimate joy we would be immersed in our work after we overcome all the hurdles on our way and reach the finishing line. Why so easily baffled? Never, let's go.

   July    7th

       Thanks to every hand and every sincere heart. With them, what else can't we accomplish? The Second Office of the Teaching Affair and the Quickware Company who helped us solve the financial problem and all the teachers who offered their hands during the whole process and Zhudong who designed the seal and Chengliang who inscribe it, we are most grateful to all of you.

   September  1st

       Here in our hands is the book. Excitement, emotion, what we are feeling inside right now we can never tell. It may be nothing in the eyes of the experts: the insights far from mature, the writings far from ease and grace and even pitted with all kinds of little bugs. But it is so special to us. You can smell the youth from every page and feel our beating hearts in every word. There lies our courage, our zeal, our perseverance, there lies the most precious page in our life. Let us all remember these days, these brilliant days. Until ever ...
